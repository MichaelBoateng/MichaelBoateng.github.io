title: Giving Back Through STEM — Volunteering with STEM Santa Fe
---

During my summer research internship at **Los Alamos National Laboratory**, I had the privilege of volunteering with [STEM Santa Fe](https://stemsantafe.org/) — an organization dedicated to advocating for, developing, and providing STEM programming, mentoring, and resources for all youth, especially underrepresented groups, to realize their potential and expand their opportunities in a dynamic world.

</div>

I had the opportunity to lead two hands-on workshops across northern New Mexico:

- **STEM Volunteer: Building Laser Harps with Electronics** — Santa Fe Community College, NM, USA
- **STEM Volunteer: Designing Guitars with Electronics** — Northern NM College, Española, NM, USA

These sessions brought together K–12 students to build working musical instruments powered by electronics — sparking curiosity and creativity at the intersection of physics, engineering, and art.

> *STEM Santa Fe envisions a world filled with analytical citizens exploring complex issues for the betterment of society.*

It was incredibly rewarding to engage directly with the local New Mexico community and help young students see themselves as engineers and makers. Experiences like these remind me why outreach is just as important as the research itself.

Learn more at [stemsantafe.org](https://stemsantafe.org/).
